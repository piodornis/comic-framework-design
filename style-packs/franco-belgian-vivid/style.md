> **Approval scope:** `APPROVED` here means approved as a reusable framework Style Pack. A comic project must still explicitly select or approve this style for its own design layer.

## Intent

Create an unmistakably illustrated comic look with strong graphic readability, bold ink contours, vivid saturated color, simplified expressive forms, and polished album-comic clarity.

This Style Pack defines visual treatment only. When transforming photographs or other existing source material, combine it with an appropriate Transformation Profile such as `photo-to-comic`.

## Visual Principles

- Make the result read immediately as an illustration rather than a lightly filtered photograph.
- Favor clear silhouettes and readable foreground/background separation.
- Keep forms lively, expressive, and simplified without becoming visually vague.
- Use polished comic composition rather than cinematic photorealism.
- Maintain strong graphic contrast.

## Linework

- Use bold, clean black outer contours.
- Use visible hand-drawn ink character in interior contours.
- Keep important shapes crisp and readable.
- Reduce noisy micro-detail when it weakens silhouette clarity.
- Use controlled hatching or texture only where it supports form or atmosphere.

## Color

Favor a highly saturated, energetic palette.

Characteristic accents may include:

- neon or vivid greens
- acid yellows
- turquoise and cyan
- hot magenta
- electric blues
- saturated purples

Use strong color separation rather than muddy blended color.

Local palettes may vary when subject identity or project canon requires different colors.

## Shape Language

- favor clear, simplified readable masses
- preserve distinctive silhouette cues
- use rounded or graphic shape exaggeration where appropriate
- keep anatomy and objects recognizable unless deliberate caricature is requested

## Rendering and Shading

- favor flat color regions
- use clear cel-shading blocks
- use stylized highlights
- use graphic shadow shapes
- allow controlled ink texture and hatching
- avoid soft photographic rendering as the dominant treatment

## Character Treatment

- simplify facial rendering while keeping expressions readable
- emphasize expressive eyes, brows, mouth shapes, and body language where appropriate to the subject
- preserve defining costume, markings, accessories, or identity cues required by the project
- maintain clean separation between character silhouette and background

## Environment Treatment

- simplify the scene into readable graphic layers
- preserve important architecture and scene-defining objects
- use color and contour to separate depth planes
- allow colorful atmospheric accents without obscuring spatial understanding

## Composition

- favor clear focal hierarchy
- keep foreground, subject, and background relationships easy to read
- support playful but controlled comic-album composition
- avoid visual clutter that competes with the subject

## Consistency Anchors

Across a sequence or batch, keep stable:

- contour hierarchy
- saturation level
- cel-shading logic
- shadow graphicness
- simplification level
- silhouette readability
- background detail density

## Variation Allowance

May vary:

- local accent palette
- atmospheric effects
- hatching density
- background simplification
- highlight intensity

as long as the core graphic language remains recognizable.

## Avoid

- subtle photo-filter results that still read primarily as photography
- painterly soft-edge rendering as the dominant visual language
- franchise-specific character designs or copied copyrighted visual elements
- unnecessary logos, speech balloons, frames, or text unless requested by the project
- imitation of a named living artist
- identity-distorting caricature unless explicitly requested

## Known Failure Modes

Watch for:

- excessive neon color overpowering canonical costume or object colors
- too much texture reducing clean readability
- facial simplification losing recognizability
- background detail competing with the main subject
- black outlines becoming uniformly heavy rather than hierarchical
